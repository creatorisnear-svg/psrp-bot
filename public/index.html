Who we are

Roleplay that holds its weight

We are not a deathmatch server with a police job bolted on. Scenes here have consequences that carry. An arrest means paperwork and a record, a wrecked car means a repair bill, and a reputation follows your character around the city.

The staff team plays on the same server under the same rules. Reports are handled in-game and logged, priorities are called publicly, and the area of patrol is posted so you always know where the city is live.

What you get

Built for people who stay

Every system here exists to make a long-running character worth playing.

Whitelisted departments

Police, fire and EMS run on applications and training, not a job menu. You earn the badge.

An economy with friction

Wages, fuel, repairs, rent and licences. Money moves slowly enough that owning something means something.

Custom scripts, not a preset

Our HUD, chat, spawn and staff tools are written for this server, not a downloaded pack with the serial filed off.

Live priority system

Each region runs open, on hold or on cooldown, posted in-game and in Discord, so scenes never collide.

Staff who actually play

Ranks come from the Discord, reports are logged, and no one on the team gets an in-character advantage.

A mapped, lived-in city

Custom postals, a satellite map and location tooling so calls can actually be found and dispatched.
